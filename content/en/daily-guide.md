# Rules for the Phenix Alliance

To grow fast and keep the alliance strong, every member of **Phenix Alliance** must follow these rules and understand our strategy.

## Main Rules

### 1. Pressing Help
**Always press the Help** button (the shaking hands icon) whenever you see it. This reduces the time for your allies' buildings and research. In return, they will help you!
If you are complacent, or "forget" to press the help button so will do more and more players and this becomes a real problem.

The strength of an alliance is measured by the number of members who are active and contribute to the alliance. All the other rewards are derived from this fact.
As a matter of fact inactive players are kicked out of every top alliance without any regrets. 

### 2. Join rallies

Actively join rallies and support your peers. This will help you earn rewards and also help you in return. The top players join these rallyes without asking them to do so, because they know that makes you grow personally and get appreciation for the teamwork. There are many events that need rallies to be successful.

Being a teamplayer is a social skill that you can take back in your life, either in your career, or relationship. This game provides a medium for you to practice your own skills, to develop your personality and to make new friends. Believe me, this is a reward in itself, far beyond orange fragments and diamonds.

### 3. Say hello
 
Be polite generally and use "Good morning", "Thank you", "Please" and "Good night". We are 100 players tops but most of us become friends and we care about each other. You will see how coincidentally identical the top players are organizing things, planning actions and how many qualities and defects we have in common. OCD is one of those "qualities". We also share a sick sense of humor and we love to laugh together. 

### 4. Don't be racist, sexist or a pain in the ass

We mean it, if you cant't behave like an adult, we will ask you to join the phe2, the kindergarden of the Phenix Alliance. But even there you will be asked to leave if you don't behave like a horny teenager that lost his wifi connection during mating season.

Also if you think in your nations superiority far too much, we will recommend you to join some banned organizations.

Here anyone is welcome, but only the normal humans feel like home.

> Pain in the ass = someone that causes trouble, or has only a full list of complaints but very little to show off in the Alliance duel. 


### 5. Danger Lurks (Boomers)

- **Auto Team-up**: Use the "Auto" toggle when joining or starting rallies to ensure slots are filled quickly. Use both options Boomer and Berserk Zombie.
- Join rallies even when you don't play actively makes sure you get **Alliance gifts** consisting of Wood, Food, Electricity, Troop Healing and Troop Training.
- **This option expires after 12 hours**. Renew it twice a day.

### 6. Prime Mining
Use the **Prime Mining Zone** bonus in the **Mines Melee**.
- It makes your extraction rate at 120%
- 4 hours of mining are guaranteed. That means the first 4 hours no one can beat your teams and finish their mining.
- Focus on Crystal mines as they give you Hero XP points.
- Zent mining is used for Research in the Laboratory.
- Use the free detection to find a free mine, or beat a team from other server to their mined resources.
- If you atack a mined occupied form a player of the server 347 you are going to be attacked by them later! Repeat this offense and you will be zeroed, or can find a new alliance to join.
- This happened already!
  
- **Etiquette**: Try to occupy empty mines. **Do not steal** missions from other players of server 347, from any alliance!


### 7. Treasure rules

- **Never march inside the terasure directly**. Pick a place near the treasure and march there fist! This is not negotiable, it is mandatory for all players!
- **Wait for the Go Signal**: Do not enter the treasure until the leader gives the **GO** command. This comes after all players on march arrive to the treasure. 
- **Jump Inside**: Once signaled "Go" or "Go tresor", move your tank inside.
- **Reinforcements**: Pick the ally base closest to the treasure, **reinforce** them instead of marching directly near the treasure. This reduces the waiting time for all players.

### 8. Trade trucks

- **Do NOT hit Trade Trucks** belonging to players from our home server (**347**). 
- We maintain a Non-Aggression Pact (NAP) with our neighbors; attacking their trucks will cause you to be atacked by them as repay, on multiple offenses, you the second offense you will be attacked until your base explodes and you loose all your troops and resources.
- **Steal!** trucks from other players of rival server. This is announced every Monday morning or Sunday evening. You steal their cargo by finding a orange truck form that server number. Or by searching the number of that server. Steal all 4 trucks, every day! 

### 9. Bounty missions

- **Do not steal** bounty missions from other players of server 347. 
- First offense is an attack, seconds gets you zeroed, being a recidivistic offender you will be educated at phe2, the kindergarden of the Phenix Alliance, in best case.
- Help your alliance members complete their missions. All 5 of them. The more you help, the less the rival server players can steal from us.
- **Steal!** bounty missions from other players of rival server. This is announced every Monday morning or Sunday evening. You steal their mission by finding a truck form that server number. Or by searching the number of that server. Steal all 5 missions, every day!
It is us or them, we need to do the same or more damage to them than they do to us. 

### 10. Alliance duel

Lastly and the most important to this alliance is the **Alliance duel**.  We are ambitious and we can't be satisfied with players that don't score points. We keep track of our performance, and our player ranking system is based on the alliance duel weekly score and loyalty, and involvement. The amount of respect, appreciation and recognition we give to each player is based on their performance in the alliance duel. Be someone, not just a NPC in this game. Some naturally become more important, because they do more for the group, as they do for themselves.

Being performant once is not enough, this must be done again and again. Nobody sleeps on their laurels, we are always looking for new ways to improve and compete, foremost with ourselves, inside the alliance. Our common duty is to do our best, to be betetr than last week, to help each other become better.

This is why you read this wiki.
