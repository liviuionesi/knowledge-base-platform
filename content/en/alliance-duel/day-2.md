# Alliance Duel: Tuesday

## 1. Overview

Day 2 (Tuesday) of the Alliance Duel revolves around **Shelter Upgrades, Recruiting Refugees, and Bounty Missions**.
To maximize points, synchronize your construction with the **Golden Hour** when Full Preparedness tasks align with the Alliance Duel.

---

## 2. Preparation

Proper preparation is the key to outscoring your opponents:

1. **Construction Timing**: Start a long building upgrade on Monday or the weekend, timing it so it completes on Tuesday after the daily reset, usually the HQ, or what is needed to update the HQ to the next level. This will give you a massive points boost right out of the gate.
2. **Refugee Recruitment**: Save your Refugee Tickets. Do not use them on any other day! Except if you casually find a Orange Troops Instructor, then get them!
3. **Bounty Missions**: Keep your refresh tickets for Bounty Missions for Tuesday and Saturday! Use as little as possible on the other days of the week. Have a reserve of at least 4000 Diaomonds left at every time. A refresh of the bounty missions costs 100 Diamonds.
4. **Hero Sophia**: update her to get more free construction time
5. **Refugee Butler**: get them to have faster construction speed
6. **Secretary of Construction**: apply for this position at the capital to have faster construction speed

---

## 3. How to Score Points

Here are the ways to earn points on Tuesday:

### 3.1 Shelter Upgrades & Construction
- **Increase Structure power by 10 points:** 4 points
- **Use 1-min Construction Speedup:** 131 points

*Strategy:* Wait for Alliance Help before using *any* speedups! Once you have max helps (or are close to the Golden Hour deadline), use your construction speedups. This task rewards both the power gained from finishing a building and the actual minutes of speedups used to get there. 
Apply often on Tuesday for the position of Secretary of Construction at the capital to have faster construction speed.

### 3.2 Refugee Recruitment
- **Recruit 1 Orange refugee:** 131.1K points
- **Recruit 1 Purple refugee:** 32.7K points
- **Recruit 1 Blue refugee:** 6.5K points

*Strategy:* Just use all your saved refugee tickets today only! We usually use the 500 Tickets option, rarely do we use the 2000 or 5000 at once. The refugees that bring the most nebefits in the game are (max importance is first):
1. Assaulter, Shooter, or Rider Instructors - these reduce the training time.
2. Diplomat: gives more minutes per each help (44 x 12 min = 9H speedup in construction, research and healing)
3. Scientist, gives faster research
4. Butler, gives faster construction
5. The rest are less important. 

### 3.3 Bounty Missions
- **Perform 1 orange [Bounty Missions]:** 196.6K points x 8 

*Strategy:* Focus your refresh Tickets on obtaining Orange Bounty Missions. When you get one, send the team for it, then use refresh tickets again. Or diamonds if you run out of the tickets. On Saturday this must be done again.   

### 3.4 Premium Options
- **Buy packs containing Diamonds [1 Diamond]:** 30 points

## 4. When to do it (Golden Hour)

The most efficient time to use your Construction Speedups is during the **Golden Hour**.
- Check the [Full Preparedness](/docs/full-preparedness) schedule.
- Look for the **Shelter Upgrade** task.
- When this task is active, use your construction speedups and finish your buildings. You will max out your Full Preparedness chests while scoring huge points for the Alliance Duel.
