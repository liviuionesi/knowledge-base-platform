# Alliance Duel: Saturday

## 1. Overview

Day 6 (Saturday) of the Alliance Duel is the **Enemy Buster** day. This day focuses on direct combat with units, as well as utilizing Speedups, Bounty Missions, and Intercity Trades.

---

## 2. Preparation

Proper preparation can help you secure points efficiently:

1. **Save Speedups:** Saving your speedups for when they provide points is crucial.
2. **Bounty Missions & Intercity Trades:** Keep your highest quality (Orange) Bounty Missions and Intercity Trades ready to be collected or completed on Saturday.
3. **Healing and Troop Management:** Prepare your hospitals and resources for potential troop losses, depending on how aggressively you plan to fight.

---
