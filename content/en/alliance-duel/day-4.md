# Alliance Duel: Thursday

## 1. Overview

Day 4 (Thursday) of the Alliance Duel is heavily focused on **Heroes and Equipment**, specifically consuming fragments, upgrading gear, and recruiting.
To maximize points, pull your recruits and use your fragments during the **Golden Hour** when Full Preparedness tasks align with the Alliance Duel.

---

## 2. Preparation

Proper preparation is the key to winning Thursday:

1. **Recruitment Tickets**: Hoard your Prime Recruitment tickets all week. Thursday is the day to spend them!
2. **Hero Fragments**: Save all Orange, Purple, and Blue hero fragments. Do not consume them to upgrade hero skills on other days.
3. **Equipment Materials**: Save your Enhancement Alloys, Orange Skill Books, Power Cores, and Exclusive Equipment Fragments. Do not upgrade equipment on other days!

---

## 3. How to Score Points

Here are the ways to earn points on Thursday:

### 3.1 Hero Upgrades & Recruitment
- **Consume 1 Orange Hero Fragments:** 16.3K points
- **Consume 1 Purple hero fragments:** 5.7K points
- **Consume 1 Blue hero fragments:** 1.6K points
- **Perform 1 Prime Recruits:** 2.1K points

*Strategy:* This is where you blow your Prime Recruitment tickets. Using tickets will naturally give you fragments, which you then consume to upgrade your heroes, double-dipping on points.

### 3.2 Equipment & Enhancements
- **Consume 1 Orange Equipment as Promotion material:** 983.4K points
- **Spend 1 Exclusive Equipment Fragments:** 16.3K points
- **Consume 1 Power Cores:** 1.7K points
- **Consume 10 Enhancement Alloys:** 9 points
- **Consume 1 Orange Skill Books:** 32 points

*Strategy:* Promoting equipment using Orange Equipment as material drops an absolutely massive near-1-million points per use. Wait for Thursday to do *all* of your gear enhancements.

### 3.3 Premium Options
- **Buy packs containing Diamonds [1 Diamond]:** 30 points

---

## 4. When to do it (Golden Hour)

The most efficient time to use your Prime Recruits and Hero EXP is during the **Golden Hour**.
- Check the [Full Preparedness](/docs/full-preparedness) schedule.
- Look for the **Hero Initiative** task.
- When this task is active, use your resources. You will max out your Full Preparedness chests while simultaneously putting massive points on the board for the Alliance Duel.
