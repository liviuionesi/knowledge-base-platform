# Guard of Order Heroes

The Guard of Order faction is the least used faction for making a team 1, but they are the best faction for making a team 2 or 3.
The top players have all 3 teams of similar strength.and win the tournament with this setup.

### Faction Relationships

On equal team strength: 
- **Counter Advantage**: Guard of Order heroes counter the **Wings of Dawn** faction. 
- **Strategic Weakness**: Guard of Order heroes are countered by the **Blood Rose** faction.
