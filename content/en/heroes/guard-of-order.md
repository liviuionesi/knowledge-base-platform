# Guard of Order Heroes

The Guard of Order faction features heroes that are susceptible to the Blood Rose faction but powerful in their own right.

### Faction Relationships
- **Strategic Weakness**: Guard of Order heroes are countered by the **Blood Rose** faction.

On equal strength, the faction type provides a significant advantage in combat.
