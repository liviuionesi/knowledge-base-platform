# Blood Rose Heroes

The heroes from the Blood Rose faction are used in the team 1 by the most the players.
There is a synergy between the heroes (Selena & Oliveira) from the Blood Rose faction and the Assaulter troops. So if you have season S1 heroes (Selena & Oliveira) you will get stronger troops from the Assaulter faction.

### Faction Relationships

On equal team strength: 
- **Counter Advantage**: Blood Rose heroes counter the **Guard of Order** faction. 
- **Strategic Weakness**: Blood Rose heroes are countered by the **Wings of Dawn** faction.
