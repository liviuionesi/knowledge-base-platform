# Wings of Dawn Heroes

The heroes from the Wings of Dawn faction are used in the team 2 or 3 by the most the players. If you are early in the game, it is recommended to use the heroes from the Wings of Dawn faction in the team 1, since they have an advantage later.
There is a synergy between the heroes (Scarlett & Nyx) from the Wings of Dawn faction and the Shooter troops. So if you have team seasonS1 heroes (Scarlett & Nyx) you will get stronger troops from the Shooter faction.

### Faction Relationships

On equal team strength: 
- **Counter Advantage**: Wings of Dawn heroes counter the **Blood Rose** faction. 
- **Strategic Weakness**: Wings of Dawn heroes are countered by the **Guard of Order** faction.
