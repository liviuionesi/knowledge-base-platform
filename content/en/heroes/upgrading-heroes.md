# Upgrading Heroes Guide

To achieve maximal results in combat, upgrading your heroes efficiently is crucial. This guide covers the best strategies for managing skills, star levels, and resources based on hero rarity and faction.

## 1. Skillset Upgrades

Upgrading a hero's skillset is fundamental to increasing their power. The primary source for skillset books is **Bounty Missions**.

### How to Get Skillset Books
The most efficient way to get skillset books from Bounty Missions is to use:
*   **1 Blue Hero**
*   **Rest Orange and Purple Heroes**

This combination is the "perfect way" to secure the necessary books for your skillset upgrades.

### Timing for Skillset Books
- **Blue and Purple Books**: Can be used **anytime**, as they do not provide points for any specific events.
- **Orange Books**: Must be used **EXCLUSIVELY on Thursdays** to capitalize on event points.

### Skill Prioritization
*   **For Purple and Blue Heroes**: Focus on maxing out the **3rd and 4th skills** first once they reach 5 stars.
*   **Team Development**: Once your main team's purple heroes have their 3rd and 4th skills maxed, start maxing their **1st and 2nd skills** one by one.

## 2. Star Levels and Fragments

Star levels (4★, 5★, and beyond) significantly boost base attributes. This requires Hero Fragments.

### Timing for Fragments
- **Thursday Only**: Fragments must be used **only on Thursdays** and saved during all other days of the week to maximize event rewards.

### Where to Get fragments
- **VIP Shop & Time-Limited Shop**: The easiest way to get Purple and Blue fragments.
- **Radar & Full Preparedness Events**: These events regularly reward both skillset books and fragments.
- **Gifting Roses**: Gifting roses to other players gives you **6 Orange Fragments**.
- **Zombie Saga & Camilla's Shop**: You can buy extra fragments here using **Stained Coins**.

## 4. Optimization Strategy

The most effective progression path for most players follows these steps:

1.  **Phase 1: Max Purples & Blues**: Goal is to have all Purple and Blue heroes at **5 stars** with their **3rd and 4th skills maxed**.
2.  **Phase 2: Refine Main Team**: Gradually max the **1st and 2nd skills** of the purple heroes in your primary team.
3.  **Phase 3: Transition to Orange**: While using your maxed-out purple team, steadily develop your **Orange Heroes** in the background. Once they are sufficiently upgraded, they should replace the purple heroes in each team for ultimate power.

## 5. Faction Specifics

Always remember to maintain a balance across your teams:
- **Blood Rose**: Great for Team 1 (Assaulter synergy).
- **Wings of Dawn**: Strong counters to Blood Rose.
- **Guard of Order**: Best for Team 2 and 3, countering Wings of Dawn.

By following this roadmap, you ensure that your resources are spent wisely and your power grows consistently across all factions.
