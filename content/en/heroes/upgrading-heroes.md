# Upgrading Heroes Guide

To achieve maximal results in the game, upgrading your heroes efficiently is crucial. This guide covers the best strategies for managing skills, star levels, and resources based on hero color and faction.

## 1. Skills Upgrades

Upgrading a hero's skills is fundamental to increasing their power. The primary source for skillset books is **Bounty Missions**. The second source is **Full Preparedness**.

### How to Get Skillset Books
The most efficient way to get skillset books from Bounty Missions is to use:
*   **Send for existing Orange and Purple Missions first**  until only the **Blue Missions** are left.
*   **Use a refresh ticket** to get either **Orange** or **Purple** Missions and start those.
*   **The last mission can be blue**, it takes many refresh ticket to get the last one to be of a different color.
*   **Tuesday and Saturday** are the days when you must do orange bounty missions only!

This combination is the "perfect way" to secure the necessary books for your skillset upgrades.

### Consuming the Skillset Books
- **Blue and Purple Books**: Can be consumed **anytime**, as they do not provide points for any specific events.
- **Orange Books**: Must be consumed **EXCLUSIVELY on Thursdays** to capitalize on Hero Initiative in Allaince Duel
 
### Skill Prioritization
*   **For Purple and Blue Heroes**: Focus on maxing out the **3rd skill** first, once they reach 4 stars the 4th skill is maxed.
*   **Team Development**: Once the purple heroes in your team 1 have their 3rd and 4th skills maxed, start maxing their **1st and 2nd skills** one by one.
*   Repeat for the second and third team the same process, first max the 3rd skill then the 1st and 2nd skills.
* **For Orange heroes** focus on maxing all skills as they are all important. I rarely have enough hero fragments to improve the star level of more than one orange hero and the skills books are abundant to max each skill immediately on Thursday.
  
## 2. Star Levels and Fragments

Star levels (4★, 5★ and the Hall of Fame) significantly boost base attributes. This requires Hero Fragments.

### Usage of Fragments
- **Thursday Only**: Fragments of each color must be used **only on Thursdays** and saved during all other days of the week to maximize event rewards.

### Where to Get fragments
- **VIP Shop & Time-Limited Shop**: The easiest way to get discounted Purple and Blue fragments.
- **Radar & Full Preparedness Events**: These events regularly reward both skillset books and fragments.
- **Gifting Roses**: Gifting roses to other players gives you **6 Orange Fragments**.
- **Zombie Saga**: You can get many fragments from this event, of each color.  
- **Camilla's Shop**: You can buy extra fragments here using **Stained Coins**.

## 4. Strategic Upgrade Path

Upgrading heroes in the right order can significantly accelerate your base progression and combat effectiveness. Also the heroes are made available by the game in a certain order and season 1 and 2 heroes must be bought, so it is important to upgrade who you have available. Here is the recommended path:

### Phase 1: Resource & Training Foundation (Early Game)
Your first priority is to stabilize your economy and troop production.
1.  **Blue Heroes**: Max **Audrey**, **Angelina**, and **William** to 5★ first. Their combined training speed bonuses are essential for building a large army early on.
2.  **Gathering Blues**: Upgrade **Natalie** (Food), **Giselle** (Wood), and **Athena** (Electricity) as needed when resources become a bottleneck.
3.  **Katrina (Orange)**: Max her to maximize exploration EXP and fuel, ensuring your heroes level up faster.
4.  **Laura (Orange)**: Maximize radar efficiency for extra resources and exploration chests.
5.  **1st & 2nd Skill Maxing**: Only after your main utility and global effects are maxed should you focus on individual combat skills (1st and 2nd) for your primary team.
6.  **Vivian (Purple)**: Max her to 5★ to unlock the global damage buff against creeps. This makes farming resources much More efficient. She gets the most fragments in the early game so anyone can max her.

### Phase 2: Economic Powerhouse (Utility Focus)
Once your basic economy is running, focus on the heroes that reduce costs and increase development speed.
1.  **Sophia (Orange)**: Her construction speed and resource reduction are the highest priority.
2.  **Amelia (Orange)**: Follow closely with research speed to keep your tech competitive.
3.  **Chinatsu (Orange)**: Essential for Alliance Duel. Maximizing her will significantly increase the rewards you get from weekly events.


### Phase 3: Battlefield Supremacy (Combat Support)
After your economy is strong, focus on specialized combat buffs.
1.  **Christina (Purple)**: A MUST for City Wars. Her demolition time reduction is critical for successful sieges.
2.  **Maria (Purple)**: Great hybrid for troop capacity and training speed.
3.  **Miranda (Purple)**: Provides global defense which helps in all combat scenarios.
4.  All purple heroes and blue heroes can reach 5 stars by the time you reach HQ level 25. 

### Phase 4: Combat Orange Heroes.  
Focus on the hero that matches your main troop type:
    *   **Selena/Oliveira** for Assaulters.
    *   **Nyx/Scarlett** for Shooters.
    *   **Sakura/Evelyn** for Riders.
*   About when you reach HQ level 26 you should phase out the usage of purple heroes in your teams and have only orange heroes as your main two teams. Most of the orange heroes have 4 stars in team 1, and orange equipment is complete on team 1.


### Phase 5: Season 2 Orange Heroes
This phase is about having each team be a 5 out of 5 orange and of the same faction. You have then the optimal buff from the orange heroes. No purple hero should be used in this phase.
